Why HMM continuity is ~0.89 and not 1.0: the metric counts only DIRECTLY adjacent arc changes. At 1 Hz a car can
  pass a short arc (< ~15 m) between two GPS points, so consecutive predictions are connected through that arc
  (the transition used the shortest path) but are not adjacent themselves. Gaps restart the chain, also breaking adjacency.
